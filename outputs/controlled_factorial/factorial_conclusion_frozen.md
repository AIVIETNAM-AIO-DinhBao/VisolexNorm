# Frozen controlled-factorial conclusion

This conclusion is bound to `controlled_factorial_protocol.json`,
`controlled_factorial_summary.json`, and `controlled_factorial_artifacts.zip` by the SHA-256
values in `factorial_conclusion_frozen.json`.

Under the frozen protocol—three seeds and the lowest-Dev-loss checkpoint available within each
three- or eight-epoch horizon—**L8** (expanded pool, eight-epoch budget) was the strongest
evaluated factorial configuration on Dev. The expanded pool did not improve selected performance
at the three-epoch horizon, but its duration gain was larger than the small pool's, giving a
positive pool-by-duration interaction.

Terminal epoch-8 sensitivity attenuated L8-S8 to near zero. Accordingly, this is a conclusion
about the expanded pool **under the frozen Dev-loss checkpoint-selection protocol**, not proof of
a large unconditional terminal-epoch pool effect. The already observed ViLexNorm Test remains
descriptive only. C-max20-ES is exploratory optimization and cannot revise this conclusion.
